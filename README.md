## A Little Marvel Trivia

Did you know that Tony Stark's first arc reactor was originally built by Howard Stark and Anton Vanko? Tony later improved the technology and used it to keep shrapnel away from his heart. Interestingly, the device that saved his life also became one of the defining symbols of Iron Man.
